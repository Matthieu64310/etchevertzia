Welcome

We welcome you warmly to our family home in the Basque Country. Our wish is to share with family and friends our love and vision for this special place.

A bit of history...

The house Etchevertzia ("the other house" in Basque) holds its name for being the sister house of Pascoulinea ("the house of Pascal"). Both date to around 1650 and are among the oldest houses in the Serres neighbourhood.

In 1924, François de Carbonnel acquired the farm Pascoulinea and surrounding farmland following the destruction of the family home in the Somme in 1916.

In 1997, his grandson (also François) was encouraged by his wife Laurence "Lolo" to acquire Etchevertzia to pass on this new home to their descendants. The grand-children and 5th generation have now implanted their roots.

Community and nature, our passions

Since 1980, over 1000 trees were planted with the passion to protect the local biodiversity of this 10-hectare land. The house and garden are maintained and embellished by long-lasting partnerships with local artisans.
